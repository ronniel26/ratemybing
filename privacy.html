Privacy Policy

RMP Lookup for Binghamton · Effective October 28, 2026

RMP Lookup for Binghamton ("the extension") shows RateMyProfessors ratings next to instructor names in Binghamton University's class registration tools (Banner and College Scheduler). This policy explains what data the extension handles. In short: it collects nothing about you.

What the extension does not collect

The extension does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. That includes your name, B-number, login credentials, schedule, grades, browsing history, and location. It has no analytics, tracking, or advertising, and the developer runs no servers that receive data from it.

What is sent, and where

When an instructor's name appears on a Banner registration page (ssb.cc.binghamton.edu) or in College Scheduler (binghamton.collegescheduler.com), the extension sends that instructor's name to RateMyProfessors (ratemyprofessors.com) to look up their public rating. Nothing about you is included in that request. RateMyProfessors' handling of requests is covered by its own privacy policy.

What is stored on your computer

The extension saves one value in Chrome's local extension storage: RateMyProfessors' ID for Binghamton University, so it doesn't have to look it up again. Ratings it fetches are cached in memory only and are discarded when the browser closes. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.

Where it runs

The extension only reads pages on ssb.cc.binghamton.edu and binghamton.collegescheduler.com, and only the instructor names shown there. It does not run on any other website.

Permissions

  • storage: saves the Binghamton school ID described above.
  • contextMenus: adds the right-click "Look up on RateMyProfessors" option for highlighted names.
  • Access to ratemyprofessors.com: needed to fetch ratings.

Affiliation

This is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Binghamton University, Ellucian, College Scheduler, or RateMyProfessors.
